Mawqoot Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · App ID com.hishamhikami.mawqoot

In short: Mawqoot keeps everything on your phone.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We never receive your prayer log, habits or location: they stay on your phone.
  • Only while you use live translation, the speech is sent in short pieces to be turned into text and translated; it is not recorded or kept.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Who we are

Mawqoot is a prayer and habit app built by Hisham Hikami. In this policy, "we" means the developer and "the app" means Mawqoot for Android and iOS.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or log in the app is saved only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • your prayer log (prayed, in the masjid, sunnah, Witr and so on), habits and streaks;
  • your answers to the starting questions and your stage on "Your path";
  • excused days, the Friday checklist, saved reminders and your settings;
  • in Ease, what you are going through, your own steps and your duʿāʾ list;
  • live translations you chose to save, and your live translation settings.

We can't see, access or recover any of it.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your phone's location to calculate prayer times and to choose the usual calculation method for your country. The calculation happens on your phone, without internet. If you don't allow location, the app uses the prayer times of Makkah and works normally.

To show your city's name, the app asks your phone's built-in location service to turn the coordinates into a place name. On Android this service is provided by Google and may send the coordinates to Google over an encrypted connection; on iPhone it is provided by Apple. This is covered by Google's or Apple's own privacy policy. The app doesn't send your location anywhere else and doesn't keep a history of it.

Live translation and the microphone

Live translation turns a talk, such as the Friday khutbah, into text in your language. It uses the microphone only while its live screen is open and you have tapped Start, and stops when you stop it, leave the screen or leave the app.

  • The app cuts the speech at the speaker's pauses into pieces of a few seconds and sends each piece, over an encrypted connection, to our server (api.halacreative.com). The server sends it to OpenAI, which turns it into text and translates it, and the text and translation come back to your phone.
  • Our server doesn't store the audio, the text or the translation, and doesn't log them. OpenAI processes them for us as a service provider: it doesn't use data sent through its API to train its models, and under its API data controls it may keep some requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse (OpenAI's data controls).
  • To count each phone's free minutes, the app sends an anonymous phone ID: a one-way code made from Android's ID for this app, from which the real ID can't be recovered. Our server keeps only this code and the number of seconds used each month, for this month and last; older records are deleted automatically. It is not linked to your name, email, location or anything you log.
  • Like any website, our server sees your phone's internet address when it connects; it doesn't keep it, except for a short-lived count used to block abuse.
  • A translation is kept only if you tap "Save the translation" at the end; it is then stored on your phone, and you can delete it at any time.

Notifications

Prayer reminders, the daily reminder after Fajr and the other reminders you can turn on (such as the Friday reminder for live translation) are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server of ours is involved. Reminders never show what you have chosen in Ease; a duʿāʾ habit appears only as "Your duʿāʾ".

Backups

You can save a backup file and choose where it goes, for example Google Drive or an email to yourself. The app doesn't upload it anywhere by itself. Whoever stores the file (such as your cloud provider) handles it under their own policy.

Feedback by email

"Send feedback by email" on the About page opens your own email app. If you send the message, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply and to improve the app. We don't share it.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we don't sell any data. The only service that receives data from the app on our behalf is OpenAI, for live translation, as described above.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, and all of its data is deleted. There is no copy anywhere else, except backup files you saved yourself. If you used live translation, our server's record of minutes used (the anonymous phone ID and seconds) is deleted automatically after about two months; to have it deleted sooner, write to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
